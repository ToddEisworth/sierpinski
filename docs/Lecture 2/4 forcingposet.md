# A Notion of Forcing

Fix \(i,j<\sigma\) and the vertex condition given by the splitting lemma.

A condition in \(\mathbb Q^*\) is a pair \((p,s)\), where \(p\in\mathbb P\), \(s\in[B]^{<\omega}\), and:

* each vertex condition \(p\restriction b_{\{\alpha\}}\), for \(\alpha\in s\), is splittable;
* \(p\restriction b_{\{\alpha,\beta\}}\) decides \(\dot d(\alpha,\beta)\) as either \(i\) or \(j\);
* after exchanging its two vertex conditions, there is an interior condition completing them to a segment condition that also decides \(\dot d(\alpha,\beta)\) as either \(i\) or \(j\).

The spare interior condition need not be compatible with \(p\).

Set

$$
(q,t)\leq(p,s)
$$

when \(q\leq p\) and \(s\subseteq t\).


---

This poset $\mathbb{Q}^*$ is in the ground model, and is forcing equivalent to a two-step iteration $\mathbb{P}*\dot Q$.

We will show two things:

- $\mathbb{Q}^*$ is Knaster (which implies $\dot Q$ is ccc in the $\mathbb{P}$-extension), and

- in $V^\mathbb{P}$, the forcing $\dot Q$ adjoins an uncountable $A\subseteq B$ such that for every $s\in [A]^2$,  $\dot d(s)\in \{i, j\}$
