For every $\gamma<\omega_1$, the set

$$
D_\gamma=\{(p,a)\in\mathbb Q^*:s^a\setminus\gamma\neq\varnothing\}
$$

is dense. Hence the generic set $H$ is unbounded in $B$, and therefore uncountable.

For Knaster, normalize an uncountable family so that two conditions $(p_0,a_0)$ and $(p_1,a_1)$ have the same finite play and coding pattern, their sets form a $\Delta$-system, and $p_0\cup p_1$ is a Cohen condition containing no data on the new cross-faces.

Regard $p_0\cup p_1$ as II’s coherent strengthening of the common system. I’s strategy responds at a higher level, splitting every old maximal node. Keep one descendant for each root element, and assign the two descendants of each old code to the corresponding non-root elements of $s^{a_0}$ and $s^{a_1}$. The new system extends both old certificates and decides every mixed $n$-element subset. Since the new cross-face interiors are disjoint from $p_0\cup p_1$, their union is a common extension of the two conditions.
